01The House

Not a hotel, exactly. More like the home of friends who happen to cook beautifully.

The Bedelle is a Victorian-style house in Runda — Nairobi's most discreet suburb — turned, with care, into seven suites and a garden you will not want to leave.

There is no lobby, no marble check-in, no queue. There is a gate, a long drive, a swimming pool that catches the afternoon, and a kitchen that does not stop. The wifi is fast. We mention this so you can stop thinking about it.

Kambua & Robert, Hosts
02The Suites

03A Day Here

Four unhurried hours.

i.

Morning

Breakfast on the terrace — full English, Continental, or simply the guacamole the long-stay guests rave about. Coffee, juice, the morning paper. The garden does the rest.

ii.

Midday

The pool is at its bluest around noon. Lunch is set under the trees, or by the water if the sun is kind. The menu changes daily — the chef does not believe in repetition.

iii.

Afternoon

A walk in Karura Forest, fifteen minutes away. Or shopping at Village Market. Or Two Rivers, if you must. Or, very reasonably, nothing at all.

iv.

Evening

Dinner is candlelit, indoors or on the terrace. The wine list is genuinely long. A nightcap by the pool. The neighborhood is quiet by ten — by design.
